# Contributing to Accountant

Bug reports, testing, questions, and improvements are welcome. Accountant is
developed and maintained by Oleksandr Mazur. The public source is provided under
the [Accountant Source Available License](LICENSE), with
[earlier MIT rights preserved](docs/Licensing.md).

## Reports and suggestions

Open an issue describing the problem, expected behavior, and relevant steps.
Reports and feature suggestions without contributed code, documentation, or
artwork do not require a copyright assignment.

## Code, tests, documentation, and artwork

1. Read the license. You may use the app privately for personal, noncommercial
   purposes, and may fork, build, test, and prepare improvements for this
   project. Keep ownership notices and identify your fork as unofficial.
2. Keep the change focused. Follow the [development guidance](README.md#development-philosophy)
   and [app testing instructions](docs/AppTesting.md). For core changes, run
   `swift test`; for app changes, include the relevant Xcode validation.
3. Open a pull request explaining the change and how you checked it. List any
   copied material, dependencies, assets, and their licenses. Do not submit work
   owned by an employer or another person without the actual rights holder's
   authorization. Disclose generated material and its source where applicable;
   do not represent it as copyright you own if you cannot establish that.
4. Before original work from another author is merged, complete the
   [Contributor Copyright Assignment](docs/ContributorAssignment.md), identifying
   the contribution by pull request and exact commit hashes. The rights holder
   and Oleksandr Mazur must sign it. If the contribution changes after signing,
   update the identified scope before merging.

By submitting original work for review, you give the maintainer the limited
permission stated in the license to retain, inspect, build, run, and test the
proposed contribution, directly or through CI and other build or test services,
solely to review it and decide whether to accept it. This does not transfer
copyright or permit the maintainer to merge, distribute, release, or reuse the
contribution in another product. Those activities require the signed assignment
or other separate permission from the contribution's rights holder.

Send signed agreements to [sqcode06@gmail.com](mailto:sqcode06@gmail.com).
Keep signatures and personal contact details out of public issues and commits.
The maintainer records verification in the pull request without publishing
the agreement. No assignment is required for work the owner already owns.

## What the assignment means

The assignment is intended to transfer transferable copyright in the identified
original contribution to Oleksandr Mazur so that the owner can maintain,
modify, distribute, sell, and relicense the app without returning to each
contributor for permission. Attribution and any rights that cannot legally be
transferred are addressed in the agreement.

A pull request, a commit signature, a `Signed-off-by` line, or the pull request
checklist is not a substitute for the signed assignment. There is no automatic
copyright transfer on submission, and no promise to accept or release a change.

The assignment does not take ownership of unrelated work or separately
identified third-party material. Third-party material needs its own compatible
license and notices; the maintainer must approve its inclusion. Existing MIT
grants are not revoked by a new assignment.

## Before merging

The maintainer checks the contribution's authorship and third-party notices,
the applicable validation, and the signed assignment covering every original
contribution included in the final change. This is a manual requirement; the
pull request template does not enforce it automatically.
