Version 1.0. Template for an identified contribution to
<https://github.com/sqcode06/accountant-app>.

This is a working legal template. A qualified lawyer should adapt it to the
parties' jurisdiction and circumstances before it is used. An unsigned copy in
the repository does not transfer anyone's rights. Complete the contribution
schedule and have both parties sign the same agreement.

## Agreement

1. **Assignment.** Upon both parties signing this agreement, Contributor hereby
   assigns to Owner all transferable copyright and related economic rights
   Contributor owns in the Contribution, worldwide, for their full duration,
   including extensions and renewals to the extent transferable. This includes
   rights to reproduce, modify, make derivative works, publish, distribute,
   make available, perform, display, sell, license, and relicense it, in source
   and compiled forms, alone or as part of the app or other works.

2. **Rights that cannot be assigned.** To the extent Contributor has relevant
   rights that cannot validly be assigned but can be licensed, Contributor grants
   Owner a worldwide, perpetual, irrevocable to the extent permitted by law,
   royalty-free, transferable, sublicensable license to exercise those rights
   for the activities above. A license under this paragraph does not transfer
   ownership of an unassignable right.

3. **Patents.** Contributor grants Owner and recipients authorized by Owner a
   worldwide, perpetual, royalty-free, nonexclusive, transferable, sublicensable
   patent license to make, have made, use, offer for sale, sell, import, and
   otherwise transfer the Contribution, alone or combined with the project as
   submitted. This grant covers only patent claims Contributor owns or controls
   that are necessarily infringed by the Contribution or that combination.

4. **Attribution and moral rights.** Owner agrees to retain reasonable authorship
   credit in project history or contribution records; an in-app credit is not
   required. Contributor consents to modification, combination, publication,
   and the forms of distribution and licensing described above. To the extent
   law permits, Contributor agrees not to assert moral rights to prevent those
   activities. Rights that cannot lawfully be waived remain unaffected.

5. **Authority and exclusions.** Contributor confirms that Contributor owns the
   rights assigned and has authority to grant the licenses above. Any employer
   or other person owning those rights must sign as the actual rights holder.
   Contributor has identified excluded material and any prior licenses or
   obligations affecting the Contribution in the schedule. No rights in
   excluded material are assigned. Existing licenses granted to other people,
   including MIT licenses, are not revoked by this agreement.

6. **Use by Contributor.** Contributor may describe the contribution truthfully
   in a portfolio and exercise permissions independently available under the
   repository license or applicable law. This agreement grants no additional
   right to redistribute the app or reuse assigned code in other products.
   Contributor keeps ownership of unrelated work.

7. **Mutual commitments.** Contributor makes the transfer in exchange for Owner's
   commitment to preserve reasonable authorship credit under paragraph 4 and
   to review the identified Contribution for possible inclusion. No payment,
   revenue share, employment, or obligation to merge or release the Contribution
   is promised. Owner may distribute and relicense accepted work commercially
   or under proprietary terms, subject to preserved rights and other applicable
   licenses.

8. **Further documents and mandatory law.** Contributor will reasonably assist
   with documents needed to record or give effect to this transfer, at Owner's
   expense. This agreement does not override mandatory legal rights or eliminate
   required local formalities. Any amendment must be agreed in writing by both
   parties. For the identified Contribution, this agreement takes precedence
   over the repository's default contribution terms to the extent they conflict.
