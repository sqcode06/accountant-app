# Brand foundation

Status: adopted direction; naming remains intentionally open.

The product already has a sharper point of view than its working title suggests.
It lets someone record spending with very little ceremony, then protects the
result with an exact double-entry ledger. The identity should make that tension
memorable.

## Core idea

**A soft routine with a hard record.**

- Positioning: **Flexible budgets. Honest records.**
- Product promise: **Record now. Review later.**
- Functional proof: quick capture creates a draft; review turns it into a trusted
  accounting fact.

This is not a bank, an investing product, or an accountant in your pocket. It is
a calm personal money practice with unusually sound books underneath.

## Personality

- **Calm, not casual.** Easy to approach without being vague about money.
- **Exact, not institutional.** Precision appears where it protects the user.
- **Humane, not indulgent.** The app helps without praising, scolding, or gamifying.
- **Quietly capable.** Features should prove the claim; copy should not boast.

## Voice

Prefer plain verbs and observable facts.

| Prefer | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Record spending | Log a transaction |
| Awaiting review | Unposted journal entries |
| Over the limit by €12 | You blew your budget |
| Nothing is final until you confirm it | AI-powered financial control |

Sentence case is the default. Money is displayed precisely. Success copy is
brief; error copy says what happened and what remains safe.

## Visual identity

The mark is a pair of opposing ribbons around an open centre. It carries three
ideas without drawing a literal finance object:

1. every movement has two postings;
2. capture and review are two halves of one habit;
3. a record can be balanced without feeling rigid.

The fixed brand palette is cobalt `#2F5FF0`, warm paper `#F7F6F3`, and a small
settled-mint accent `#34D9A4`. Product themes may change the interface, but they do
not recolour the signature. This keeps the app recognisable across Cobalt, Ember,
Graphite, Indigo, Moss, and Paper.

The existing type system remains right for the brand: SF Pro in sentence case,
with bold tabular figures and generous space. Do not introduce a decorative font
just to make the product look branded.

Avoid calculators, currency signs, piggy banks, coins, bank columns, upward
charts, mascots, and generic sparkle language. They each promise a different
product.

## Naming brief

`Accountant` remains the working product name, not the final recommendation. A
future name should be:

- human and internationally pronounceable;
- credible beside precise financial data;
- broad enough for budgeting, records, and reconciliation;
- distinctive in finance and the App Store;
- compatible with the existing promise and symbol.

Do not adopt a candidate from a search result alone. Before changing the display
name, check App Store availability, domains, company registers in intended launch
markets, and relevant trademarks. Internal targets and `AccountantCore` do not
need to be renamed with the consumer brand.

## Applied surfaces

- App icon: full-bleed cobalt with the paired-ribbon mark.
- First run: mark, working name, positioning, then promise.
- Settings: a quiet signature at the end of the product rather than a promotional
  About screen.
- Daily screens: the brand is expressed by behaviour, typography, colour, and
  language; the logo does not need to occupy every navigation bar.
